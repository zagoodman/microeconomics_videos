\Fignote{This table replicates Table~\ref{tab:firststage_table} using credit-eligible video measures. Video measures count only credit-eligible video openings: a video counts only if the student did not open another video before this one could have been watched in its entirety (allowing one minute for timestamp rounding), the condition \textit{Incentive} students were told was required for credit. The rule is applied identically to all arms. Model (1) contains linear controls for midterm 1 score and year; (2) is the difference in means and standard errors calculated using the repeated sampling framework of Neyman (1923); (3) and (4) use the post-double-selection (PDS) procedure of \textcite{bch2014a} to select control variables then estimate treatment effects and standard errors. Models (2) and (4) include only students whose matched-pair did not attrite from the experiment. \textit{Control Mean} is the mean for the Control students included in models (1) and (3). \Regnote}
